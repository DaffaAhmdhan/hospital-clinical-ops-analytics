Hospital Clinical Queue, Biometric Check-In & BPJS E-Klaim Analytics

Built around real hospital IT systems I work with daily (SIMRS, BPJS Fingerprint Biometrics, DICOM/PACS, and INA-CBG E-Klaim): analyzing 8,400 patient visits across 6 departments to cut outpatient waiting time and recover stalled BPJS claims.

Key Takeaway (In Plain English)

What creates the morning polyclinic bottleneck—and how do we fix it? 59.7% of all hospital visits (5,014 out of 8,400) arrive between 07:00 and 10:00 AM, pushing Cardiology (78.6m) and Internal Medicine (75.3m) past the 60-minute Ministry outpatient SLA. Yet patients checking in via BPJS Fingerprint Biometric Kiosks wait 25.66 minutes less (46.28m, 73.3% SLA) than Manual Counter patients (71.94m, 26.06% SLA). Meanwhile, Rp 1.10B (13.14%) of BPJS claims sit in Pending / Revision for nearly 10 days due to incomplete coding or DICOM attachments.

SQL Clinical SLA & INA-CBG E-Klaim Queries (from sql/clinical_ops_queries.sql) 
-- Check-In Biometric vs Manual Counter Impact
SELECT
    check_in_method,
    COUNT(*) AS visits,
    ROUND(AVG(wait_time_mins), 2) AS avg_wait_mins,
    ROUND(100.0 * SUM(CASE WHEN wait_time_mins <= 60 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) / COUNT(*), 2) AS sla_60m_pct,
    ROUND(AVG(patient_csat), 2) AS avg_csat
FROM patient_visits
GROUP BY check_in_method;

-- BPJS Kesehatan INA-CBG Claim Revenue at Risk
SELECT
    claim_status,
    COUNT(*) AS claim_count,
    ROUND(AVG(claim_submission_days), 2) AS avg_turnaround_days,
    SUM(claim_amount_idr) AS claim_value_idr
FROM patient_visits
WHERE payer_type = 'BPJS Kesehatan'
GROUP BY claim_status
ORDER BY claim_count DESC;
